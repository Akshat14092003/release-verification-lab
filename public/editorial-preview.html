Correct behaviour

Lets an existing account sign in with the wrong password. Unknown emails also get a new session.

How to try it 5 matching checks
  1. Sign out and open the customer sign-in page.
  2. Choose the demo Customer and enter a wrong password.
  3. Try signing in. Correct behaviour rejects the credentials.

How to try it 5 matching checks
